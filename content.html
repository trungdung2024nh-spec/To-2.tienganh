🇹🇭 SONGKRAN WATER FESTIVAL 🇹🇭
🌸 ORIGIN Thailand’s traditional New Year festival.
Celebrates the beginning of a new year.
📅 TIME & LOCATION April 13–15 every year.
📍 Celebrated throughout Thailand.
💦 MAIN ACTIVITIES 💦 Water splashing for blessings.
🙏 Visiting temples and pouring water over Buddha statues.
👨‍👩‍👧 Giving New Year wishes and showing respect to elders.
💧 MEANING 💧 Washing away bad luck.
🍀 Wishing for peace and good fortune.
❤️ Strengthening family and community bonds.
👕 CLOTHING – CUSTOMS – FOOD 👕 Colorful floral clothes.
🌸 Pouring scented water as a blessing.
🍜 Pad Thai, Tom Yum, and mango sticky rice.
🌺 CONCLUSION
Songkran is a special traditional festival that represents joy, unity, and good wishes for the New Year.
