SONGKRAN FESTIVAL

THAILAND

✨ Introduction

Hello teachers and friends! Today, I would like to briefly introduce Songkran Festival – one of the most distinctive and famous cultural traditions of Thailand. ✨✨

☔ Name & Nature

First, regarding its name and nature: Songkran is the traditional New Year celebration of the Thai people, marking the beginning of a new year according to the Buddhist calendar. ✨✨

☔ Time

Second, regarding the time: The festival takes place annually from April 13 to April 15 – a time when people drive away the intense heat of summer. ✨✨

☔ Location

Third, regarding the location: The festival is held throughout Thailand, with the liveliest celebrations taking place in major cities such as Bangkok, Chiang Mai, and Phuket. ✨✨

WATER, JOY & TRADITION

☔ What to do at the festival

During Songkran, people can participate in water fights using water guns and buckets, and enjoy themselves together in the streets. In addition, visitors can visit temples, take part in traditional activities, enjoy local specialties, and immerse themselves in the lively atmosphere of the festival. ✨✨

☔ Meaning

Finally, regarding its meaning: Thai people believe that water symbolizes purity. Splashing water on one another helps wash away bad luck from the old year, brings wishes for peace and happiness in the new year, and provides an opportunity to strengthen family and community bonds. ✨✨

✨ Closing

Thank you, teachers and friends, for listening! ✨✨
