☔ SONGKRAN FESTIVAL ☔
☔ NAME & NATURE
Songkran is the traditional New Year celebration of the Thai people, marking the beginning of a new year according to the Buddhist calendar. ✨
☔ TIME
The festival takes place annually from April 13 to April 15, during the hot summer season. ✨
☔ LOCATION
Songkran is celebrated throughout Thailand, especially in major cities such as Bangkok, Chiang Mai, and Phuket. ✨
☔ ACTIVITIES
People enjoy water fights with water guns and buckets, visit temples, join traditional activities, taste local specialties, and enjoy the lively atmosphere.

☔ MEANING
Water symbolizes purity. It represents washing away bad luck, bringing peace and happiness, and strengthening family and community bonds.
